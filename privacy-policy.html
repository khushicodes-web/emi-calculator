Privacy Policy

At Global EMI Calculator, accessible from our website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document details the types of information collected and how we use it.

Log Files and Analytical Data

Global EMI Calculator follows a standard procedure of using log files. The information gathered includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any personally identifiable information.

Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, Global EMI Calculator uses cookies to store information including visitors' preferences and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. This data helps optimize user experience by customizing web page content.

Google DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google is one of our third-party vendors. It uses DART cookies to serve ads to our site visitors based on their visit to our site and other websites on the internet. Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy.

Third Party Privacy Policies

Global EMI Calculator's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. We advise you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of third-party ad servers or networks for more detailed information.
