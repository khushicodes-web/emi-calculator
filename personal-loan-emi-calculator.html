What is a Personal Loan EMI?

Because personal loans are unsecured, planning your budget using an instant personal loan emi calculator with low interest ensures you understand the complete unsecured personal loan repayment schedule before signing with any lender. A Personal Loan is an unsecured form of credit, which means you do not provide collateral or security against the borrowed sum. Because of higher lending risk, personal loan interest rates are typically higher (ranging from 10.5% to 28% p.a.) than secured loans.

Looking for alternative calculators? You can check out our Home Loan EMI Calculator or our Car Loan EMI Calculator.

Factors That Determine Your Monthly Outflow

  • Principal Amount: The actual borrowed loan capital.
  • Interest Rate: Higher rates increase the total repayment burden.
  • Tenure: Longer tenures lower the monthly EMI but increase total interest paid over time.
  • Processing Fees & Charges: One-time charges added during initial approval and loan sanctioning.

Tips to Get Lower Personal Loan Interest Rates

  • Maintain High Credit Score: A CIBIL score of 750 or above unlocks pre-approved offers at lower interest rates.
  • Avoid Multiple Loan Applications: Multiple hard credit inquiries within a short span can negatively impact your credit profile.
  • Check Foreclosure Terms: Choose lenders that allow prepayment without hefty penalty fees.
