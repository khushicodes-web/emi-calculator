What is a Personal Loan EMI?

A Personal Loan is an unsecured form of credit, which means you do not provide collateral or security against the borrowed sum. Because of higher lending risk, personal loan interest rates are typically higher (ranging from 10.5% to 28% p.a.) than secured loans.

Factors That Determine Your Monthly Outflow

  • Principal Amount: The actual borrowed loan capital.
  • Interest Rate: Higher rates increase the total repayment burden.
  • Tenure: Longer tenures lower the monthly EMI but increase total interest paid over time.
  • Processing Fees & Foreclosure Charges: One-time charges added during approval.
