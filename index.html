What is an Equated Monthly Installment (EMI)?

An Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) is a fixed payment amount made by a borrower to a lender at a specified date each calendar month. EMIs are applied to both interest and principal each month so that over a specified number of years, the loan is paid off in full.

How Does Our Online EMI Calculator Help You?

Calculating your monthly loan payments manually can be complex and error-prone. Our interactive financial tool allows you to easily compute your monthly installments for various types of loans including Home Loans, Car Loans, Personal Loans, and Education Loans globally.

Mathematical Formula for Calculating Loan EMI

The standard mathematical formula used to determine monthly EMI repayments is:

EMI = [P x R x (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N - 1]

Where P represents the Principal loan amount, R is the monthly interest rate, and N is the tenure in total number of months.
