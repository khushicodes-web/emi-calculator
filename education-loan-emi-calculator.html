Understanding Education Loan Repayment & Moratorium Period

Unlike other retail loans, Education Loans offer a unique Moratorium Period (Holiday Period), which extends across the duration of the course plus 6 to 12 months after graduation before regular full EMI repayments start.

Tax Benefits Under Section 80E

In India, the entire interest paid towards higher education loans is eligible for deduction from taxable income under Section 80E of the Income Tax Act for up to 8 consecutive assessment years with no upper cap limit.
