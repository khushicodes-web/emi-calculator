How Does a Home Loan EMI Work?

A Home Loan EMI is composed of two portions: principal repayment and accrued interest. During the initial years of a housing loan tenure, a major share of your monthly EMI payment goes towards servicing interest, while in the later years, the principal component increases.

Tax Deductions Available on Home Loans

  • Section 80C: Tax deduction up to ₹1.5 Lakh per financial year on principal repayment.
  • Section 24(b): Deduction up to ₹2 Lakh per financial year on the interest paid for self-occupied properties.
